The operating field

Connect the planned job, the committed job, and the built job.

A project field is not a folder, dashboard, or slogan. It is a controlled structure where project documents, decisions, roles, and obligations can be seen together.

Before the field

  • staleDrawing set and field condition no longer match.
  • loosePOs are committed without clear scope and schedule context.
  • openRFIs and submittals move, but the affected work does not.
  • lateCloseout starts after the job has already lost its memory.

Bearingfield action

  • detectFind missing, stale, conflicting, and unowned project signals.
  • placeAssign role-linked bearings for estimating, PM, field, engineering, and executive view.
  • routeConnect documents, decisions, cost, schedule, and responsibility.
  • produceTurn the field into buildable documentation and usable project controls.

After the field

  • clearThe estimate, budget, POs, and schedule can be read together.
  • currentThe team sees the current drawing state and linked RFI/submittal impacts.
  • ownedTasks and deliverables have visible responsibility.
  • keptCloseout and lessons learned are built while the work is still alive.
