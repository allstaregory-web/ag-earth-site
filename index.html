Services

Engineering Services Available for Scoping Now

Bearingfield Solutions supports electrical construction work from pre-construction through closeout. Services include engineering drawings, coordination support, construction documentation, point-to-point wiring documentation, and project closeout support.

01
Engineering Drawings & Contract Documents
Engineering drawing packages, coordination drawings, shop drawings, as-built documentation, schedules, and buildable contract-document support.
02
Coordination & Pre-Construction Support
Electrical construction coordination support, design-assist review, pre-construction planning, and constructability problem-solving before work reaches the field.
03
Construction Documentation
Point-to-point wiring documentation, panel schedules, termination lists, field wiring packages, and organized project documentation for installation teams.
04
Project Closeout & Engineering Support
Submittal support, field questions, revisions, closeout documentation, and practical engineering problem-solving for electrical construction projects.
